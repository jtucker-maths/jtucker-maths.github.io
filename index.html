About Me

I am a second-year PhD student of Mathematics at the Department of Pure Mathematics and Mathematical Statistics at the University of Cambridge, supervised by Henry Bradford. I am a member of Queens' College.

My primary mathematical interest is geometric group theory. Specifically, I am interested in the finiteness properties of (permutational) symmetric enrichments of groups (sometimes known as lampshufflers), and certain similar wreath-like constructions called 'halos of groups', as well as Dehn functions and their generalisations.

Before coming to Cambridge, I completed my MMath at Warwick in 2025. My thesis was "Homological Dehn Functions", supervised by Robert Kropholler.
